About me

The kind of engineer who's been in the designer's seat.

Most developers hand their work to a creative team and hope for the best. I spent nearly a decade in that creative team — building motion graphics, directing brand campaigns, and obsessing over the first 3 seconds of a video.

Then I formalised everything with an MSc in Computing at the University of Central Lancashire. The result? I write cleaner UI logic, catch UX problems before they're tickets, and ship products that hold up visually — no separate design pass needed.

Born in Nigeria. Built in the UK. Open to relocation.
